---
title: OASIS and deterministic engines
description: How responsibilities are divided between OASIS, agents, and deterministic engines.
---

OASIS is used as an agent-interaction environment, not as a general backend or calculator. The SimuMarket AI orchestrator builds structured context, creates councils, limits actions, runs rounds, stores traces, and validates artifacts.

## Council responsibilities

| Council | Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Market Analyst | Interpret evidence IDs and critique market conditions | Create new evidence |
| Customer Persona | Respond to stimuli as a synthetic persona | Claim to represent a real population survey |
| Finance | Critique assumptions and request the deterministic calculator | Invent break-even, margin, or payback values |
| Report | Compose narrative from validated artifacts | Add numbers without provenance |

Finance and scoring execute from typed inputs regardless of agent success. A council's requested volume is clamped to the user's input range. Narrative validation rejects numbers absent from valid input, evidence, finance, score, or simulation artifacts.

## Data protection

Only a structured `SimulationRequest` may leave for the provider. IDs are salted and hashed. Email addresses, phone numbers, and URLs in free text are redacted. Customer names, phone numbers, raw receipt text, and free-form notes never enter provider prompts.

## Provider integration

CAMEL/OASIS and model providers are loaded through an adapter. Each deployment selects Gemini or OpenAI and its model through environment configuration. Every generated artifact passes through the same validators before it can be used in a report.
