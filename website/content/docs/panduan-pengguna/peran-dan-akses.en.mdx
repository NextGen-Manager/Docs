---
title: Roles and access
description: Owner and cashier boundaries within each business.
---

A role belongs to a user-business pair. One user can be an owner in one business and a cashier in another.

## Owner

An owner manages the business profile, products, memberships, transaction history, analytics, reports, and exports. The owner dashboard is a composite summary across owned businesses. Per-business filters are available where business context is required.

## Cashier

A cashier works within one assigned store context. The role focuses on reading active products and recording transactions. A cashier cannot manage products, inspect cost or margin, open owner analytics, or change memberships.

## Cashier invitations

The owner creates an eight-character, single-use code that remains valid for seven days. Only a hash is stored. The owner can inspect invitation status or revoke it without receiving the raw code again.

## Enforcement

Hiding a menu is not authorisation. The API and repositories validate membership and role for every tenant-scoped resource. Another business's resource returns `404` even when the UUID exists, preventing tenant enumeration.

Access and refresh tokens are delivered through `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax` cookies. Refresh tokens are stored as hashes and rotated on use. The frontend does not store tokens in `localStorage`.
