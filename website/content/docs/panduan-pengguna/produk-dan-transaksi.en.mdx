Transaction Management uses one product master and one final transaction shape. Manual entry, batch entry, and receipt photos all end at a transaction record validated by the backend.

## Setting up products

The owner creates a product with a name, selling price, and unit cost. Product names are unique within a business. Money is stored as integer rupiah and cannot be negative.

Margin is not stored as a column. It is recomputed deterministically when the owner response is built, so it can never drift from the current price and unit cost.

A cashier can read active products and their selling prices in order to record transactions. The cashier product response contains no unit cost and no margin.

## Manual transactions

1. Select the business and product.
2. Enter quantity, time, and channel.
3. Confirm the transaction.
4. The backend computes `line_total_idr` and `gross_total_idr`.

The frontend is not an authoritative money calculator; it only formats figures the backend already computed. The backend also verifies that every product belongs to the same business before saving.

`client_reference` is optional and makes network retries idempotent. The combination of `business_id` and `client_reference` is unique when the reference is set, so a request that arrives twice still produces one transaction.

## Batch transactions

Batch entry is for sales recorded after the fact, such as a day of handwritten notes. Validation runs per row, and rejected rows are reported with their reason rather than dropped silently.

## Sales analytics

Analytics reaches `available` only after transactions span seven distinct local dates in the `Asia/Jakarta` timezone. Before that gate the status is `collecting`, every aggregate list is empty, and `observation_window` is empty. The system does not present a provisional pattern as fact.

What is available once the gate is met:

- gross sales per day;
- quantity and revenue per product;
- top and bottom products subject to minimum exposure;
- hourly distribution;
- rule-based insight.

A product that has not reached the seven-day minimum exposure since creation still appears in the sales list but is never selected as the bottom product. The reason is stated in the limitations list.

Every insight carries its observation window, the `Asia/Jakarta` timezone, and a `rule_version`. The system does not recommend removing a product on low volume alone; availability, margin, days recorded, and product age are all considered.

## From transactions to decisions

Analytics figures are computed by a deterministic engine from confirmed transactions. Unconfirmed OCR output is never counted, so an extraction error cannot corrupt the dashboard.
