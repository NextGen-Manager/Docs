---
title: Introducing SimuMarket AI
description: The problem, lifecycle, modules, and support provided to F&B MSMEs.
---

SimuMarket AI supports two stages of an F&B business journey.

## Before launch

A prospective owner provides a concept, a target location in Greater Jakarta, price, costs, capital, capacity, and expected volume. The system combines available market evidence, synthetic-persona simulation, deterministic financial calculations, and versioned scoring.

It helps answer:

> Is this F&B idea, at this price and location, reasonable to pursue, and which risks need mitigation?

## After launch

The owner configures a business and products. An owner or cashier can record sales. Once transactions cover seven distinct local dates in the `Asia/Jakarta` timezone, the system unlocks basic analytics such as daily sales, product performance, and rule-based insights.

It helps answer:

> What is selling, what has not received enough exposure, and which operational areas should be evaluated?

## Three main modules

| Module | Purpose |
|---|---|
| F&B Education | Explains licensing, pricing, inventory, and customer acquisition |
| Market Analysis | Produces a readiness report with evidence, finance, risks, and recommendations |
| Transaction Management | Manages products, transactions, receipt review, analytics, and exports |

The product lifecycle is `Learn -> Analyse -> Launch -> Record -> Understand -> Improve`.

## Four OASIS councils

- Market Analyst assesses competitor evidence and market conditions.
- Customer Persona runs synthetic-persona responses to a scenario.
- Finance critiques financial feasibility but does not act as the calculator.
- Report composes narrative from validated artifacts.

Finance, scoring, and transaction aggregates remain deterministic code responsibilities outside free-form LLM decisions.
