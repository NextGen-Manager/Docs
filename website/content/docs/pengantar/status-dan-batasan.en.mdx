This documentation deliberately distinguishes prototypes, implementation, and production validation.

## Available capabilities

- Authentication, session rotation, multi-business membership, and owner/cashier roles.
- Businesses, products, manual and batch transactions, dashboard, and transaction analytics.
- Education and its prerequisite gate.
- Asynchronous analysis pipeline, SSE, recovery leases, deterministic finance, scoring, and reporting.
- Private receipt upload, preprocessing, PaddleOCR adapter, draft review, correction, and idempotent confirmation.
- PDF reports and transaction summaries through a background worker and signed download URLs.

## Not ready for a completed claim

- The production market-evidence provider has not been selected. Production can produce missing evidence and a `partial` report.
- Rule set `lrs-v0.2-unvalidated` uses 20/25/15/40 weights, but thresholds and model quality still require expert review and calibration.
- PaddleOCR accuracy has not been evaluated on a representative pilot dataset of Indonesian MSME receipts.
- Some integrations and screens still use seed data for demonstrations.

## Understanding `partial`

`partial` means valid deterministic sections can still be shown while another component is unavailable. The failed section stays visible with a safe explanation. The system does not invent defaults, silently redistribute weights, or relabel the run as `completed`.

## Simulation is not field research

Synthetic personas help test structured reactions to scenarios. They are not a statistical representation of everyone in Greater Jakarta and do not replace interviews, observations, or direct market evidence.
